## Problem

Older adults (65+) with low digital maturity need a way to feel safe and independent online. Existing digital platforms are designed without consideration for their actual conditions leading to avoidant behavior, dependence on relatives and a shrinking digital self-confidence.

## Our solution

A platform that is built from the ground up for the elderly, not adapted afterwards but designed for them from day one. The core of our idea is to create a community for a maximum of 40 people who are matched based on interests and location. While we solve a simpler navigation on the internet, we also want to improve leisure time and reduce loneliness among the elderly.

## Features

- Automatic assignment to a community with an ML-model.
- Small-scaled community groups containing max 40 users.
- Social feed containing posts from all members.
- Chat to interact with peers. 
- Feature to create and attend community based events.
