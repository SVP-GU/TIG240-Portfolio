## Problem Space

Reddit is a social media platform consisting of millions of users who gather for conversations about things they care about. Through this, many people engage in politically active subreddits to get their view across.
However, politically charged comment sections can quickly become difficult to moderate as conversations grow, users disagree, and discussions shift from constructive debate to toxic or escalating interactions.

---

## Our Solution

The Modsignal extension is a Reddit moderation tool that helps moderators identify toxic, polarizing, or escalating users/comments in swedish political discussion threads. 
The tool is not intended to be used as an automated decision-maker, but rather as a support mechanism for moderators navigating large, political subreddits.

--

##User Flow

1. A Reddit moderator opens the extension.
2. The moderator selects a topic or subreddit view to monitor.
3. The extension displays a prioritized queue of comments and threads based on escalation risk.
4. The moderator opens a case to review the flagged content, priority level and explanation.
5. The moderator uses the queue and case views to decide which discussions need further attention.

---

## Key features

| **Priority based comment que** | A que overview with prioritized comments ranked from 0-100 based on toxicity score and foul words  |
| **Moderators decision** | Each flagged comment can be handled through four clear moderation actions: Ignore, Warn, Remove, or Escalate. |

---

## How we differ from peers

Tiveden vs. **Pieljekaise**: While Echo focuses on self-reflection in the age of social media by making users more aware of their filter bubbles and social media usage, Modsignal focuses on moderators instead, offering a supportive tool that helps them navigate large politically active subreddits and make more informed moderation decisions. 
Tiveden vs. **Sarek**: While WikiConflict helps Wikipedia readers get an insight into the editing activity behind articles, Tiveden helps Reddit moderators manage conflict inside active comment sections. Hence, Reddit readers can not get ahold of the Reddit extension.
